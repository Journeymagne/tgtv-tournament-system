Как это работает

Simulates initiative rolls with Re-roll cards, +1/-1, +2/-2, +3/-3 modifiers, and team abilities. Abilities are applied before Re-roll; +1d3 is revealed before card Re-roll decisions, and card Re-roll results are not known before both players decide.
