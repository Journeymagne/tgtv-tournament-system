# Changelog

What's new in KT Companion: the latest 10 released updates, newest first.

## 1.4.35 — 4 November 2026

- Core rules training now offers only new questions and questions you answered incorrectly on your latest attempt. If fewer than 20 remain, the session is shorter; solved questions are no longer added just to fill it.
- You can still practise any solved question in the question bank.
- Fixed tournament participants' faction selection fields: additional fields are no longer clipped.

## 1.4.33 — 31 October 2026

- Fixed errors when saving prepared rounds and deleting tournaments.
- Tournament matches and their corresponding game records now share a single result. Existing match history has been preserved.

## 1.4.32 — 30 October 2026

- Account and Studio sign-in and registration forms now follow the same rules.
- Shared styles and interface elements now update consistently across the site.

## 1.4.31 — 30 October 2026

- Made the homepage navigation more compact.
- Improved homepage, tournament and calculator descriptions for search engines.

## 1.4.30 — 30 October 2026

- Handouts now offers two combined print collections: A4 and A5. Both include WTC Open, Volkus, Tomb World, WTC Into the Dark and Octarius.
- Restored the full operative placement rule in the Octarius reference and corrected the terrain feature name to Oil Pump.

## 1.4.29 — 30 October 2026

- Added Octarius to the combined A4 PDF. The third sheet contains two A5 references you can cut out after printing.

## 1.4.28 — 30 October 2026

- Added Octarius as the fifth page of the combined A5 collection, matching the style of the other KT Companion references.

## 1.4.27 — 30 October 2026

- Achievements for learning individual teams now appear alongside other Companion achievements.
- These achievements use the corresponding team's icon in profiles, achievement lists and training progress.

## 1.4.26 — 30 October 2026

- Refined the English text in the WTC Open, Volkus, Tomb World and WTC Into the Dark references.
- Added standalone Octarius print references: A5 and A4 with two copies per sheet.

## 1.4.25 — 30 October 2026

- Added an Unsolved filter to the question bank. It shows new questions and questions you have never answered correctly.
- Combine it with course, difficulty, topic and search filters. A question leaves this selection after your first correct answer.
