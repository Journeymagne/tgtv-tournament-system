# Changelog

KT Companion release history, newest first.

## 1.8.7 — 28 November 2026

- Players and organizers can open tournaments and view the current round again in older mobile browsers.
- The page shows “Tournament not found” when the tournament is unavailable at that link. If the page fails to load, it prompts you to try again.
- Opening a tournament link now shows labels in your selected language and a start time marked in UTC on the initial card.

## 1.8.6 — 25 November 2026

- Creating a tournament now opens its overview. Each tab shows the organizer the current stage and next action. Organizers can also return directly to their draft using its link.
- After closing registration, organizers can prepare first-round pairings. If the field is too small, the tournament shows how many more players or rosters it needs and links to the entry list.
- The pairing editor shows opponents, the number of pairs and who receives the bye. Organizers can edit saved pairings before starting the tournament. Reopening registration asks for confirmation before clearing them.
- FAQ editors can search by part of a team name and add multiple teams to a question.
- Calendar cards and event details link to the organizer’s profile or the organization hosting the event.
- On phones, selecting a team in a tier list keeps the board clear for dragging. On desktop, selecting a team still opens its statistics.

## 1.8.5 — 25 November 2026

- Tournaments with configurable standings tiebreakers now offer Total Primary. It adds the Primary bonuses earned by a player or team. A higher total breaks a tie after earlier criteria.
- Type part of a team name to see matching options in alphabetical order. When reporting a tournament result, choose from the player's registered teams.
- Table layout generation now suggests unused layouts from the same Killzone set first, including edits on other tables that have yet to be saved. Organizers can still select repeated layouts manually.
- Telegram now asks for Crit Op, Tac Op and Kill Op scores in that order, followed by the Primary Op selection.

## 1.8.4 — 20 November 2026

- Save weapon profiles to your account and use them on other devices. You can delete a saved profile or import profiles previously saved in this browser.
- Manual profiles show normal and critical damage together in the characteristics column. Rules and rerolls are in the second column. Shooting situation B starts collapsed.
- General effects are in Advanced, and faction effects have their own section. Select ⓘ to read what an effect does. On phones, both sections start collapsed and open independently.
- The calculator now supports Oubliex, Survival Instincts, Domino Field, Omnissiah’s Imperative, Sturdy, Undaunted Explorers, Critical Shot, Vicious Venom, Acts of Faith with Icon of Faith, Scry Protection, Aerial Agility and the Ravener Wrecker’s Crush.
- In a two-shot sequence, you can apply Lethal and Piercing to just the first or second shot. Just a Scratch cancels one die’s normal damage. Mystic Scry Buff is now called Skry Guidance.

## 1.8.3 — 20 November 2026

- Drag teams to move them in a tier list. Click a miniature to open its statistics. On a touchscreen, touch and hold the miniature, then drag it to a tier or back to the pool.
- Download JPEG saves the title, season and all five tiers in one image. The export uses your theme, miniature size and team name settings.

## 1.8.2 — 19 November 2026

- In tier list statistics, the Season label fits on one line next to the season selector.

## 1.8.1 — 19 November 2026

- In tier lists, Shuffle reorders all unranked teams in the pool. Undo move restores their previous order.
- Choose a season in a kill team's statistics panel to see its games, win rate and top Tac Ops. The selection carries over when you open another team. Player rankings cover all seasons.
- The 2026 Q4 season is now called “2026 Q4 Dataslate”.

## 1.8.0 — 19 November 2026

- Tournaments now has a Leagues tab. A tournament page links to the standings for each league it belongs to.
- Individual standings keep a registry player's results together when their nickname changes. Each row shows event count, kill teams and a breakdown by tournament. Organization standings add points from every roster and count each doubles or team tournament once.
- Organizers can link past tournaments, award tournament points or points by place in each set of standings, and copy or customize tie-breakers. A preview shows how the standings will change before saving.
- Updated tabs, keyboard navigation, menus, tables and FAQ filters on narrow screens. Tournament management actions are grouped under Settings, and the roles list shows current organization heads.

## 1.7.3 — 19 November 2026

- Clickable organization cards preserve the UI Kit content typography and left alignment.

## 1.7.2 — 19 November 2026

- The entire organization card now opens its profile, including its logo, city and padding. Keyboard navigation and opening in a new tab remain available. Cards show the home city without a member count.

## 1.7.1 — 19 November 2026

- Calendar announcements can leave their fee and player capacity blank. Both display as to be confirmed; a fee of 0 still means free entry.
- Pending conditions survive editing and tournament creation without becoming a zero registration limit.

## 1.7.0 — 18 November 2026

- Tournaments now includes an Organizations tab: tournament providers with a name, logo, home city, description, and a roster of organizers and judges.
- The creator becomes the chief organizer and invites players with a chosen role. Organizers create tournaments and calendar events for their organization; judges run its events. Platform administrators can manage every organization and its membership.
- Organization profiles have collapsible current, upcoming and past event lists. Linked tournaments and announcements appear once, and event cards and the calendar identify their provider.
- Added ownership transfer, leaving and archiving organizations. Membership changes update access, while explicit tournament judge appointments remain independently manageable.
- The interface supports both themes and languages and mobile screens. Organization filters are aligned, and announcement times are preserved when organizers work across time zones.

## 1.6.1 — 18 November 2026

- Tournament organizers with tournament-creation permission can now add events to the Calendar.
- Administration → Players and teams now includes an Administrators tab: search users with special permissions and manage global roles, tournament organizers and judges.
- Any administrator can grant and edit permissions, assign judges and transfer tournament ownership. Only the super administrator can remove administrator status.
- Tournament staff lists show the organizer and assigned judges. Global administrators have access by default and appear in the list only when they hold a tournament role.

## 1.6.0 — 18 November 2026

- Added a Kill Team event calendar with a month view and list, mobile event cards with tournament posters, search, and Russian city autocomplete. Singles and team events have distinct colours, and tournaments you participate in have a personal marker.
- Dated tournaments now appear in the calendar automatically. Changing the start date moves the linked event without duplicates or losing announcement details. Automatically generated drafts are administrator-only until publication.
- Added WTC Doubles for TTS and in-person tournaments: two-player rosters, a dedicated pairing sequence, scoring, and fixed tiebreakers. Other formats now share a consistent tiebreaker settings layout.
- Tournament viewing and management now share one page. Administrator actions stay visible; the common header shows format, team size, and pairing type. Pending results can be opened for review directly from match lists.
- Completed tournaments open Results first, while Overview retains the final round's maps, missions, and match results. Improved warnings for assigning multiple pairs to one physical table and handling round pairings.
- Player profiles have a compact statistics row. Personal details and account controls are grouped under Settings for the owner and administrators. Achievement display and sections have been updated.
- Statistics now offers Kill Team autocomplete and correct all-time selection, with shorter Winrates section labels in both languages.

## 1.5.38 — 17 November 2026

- The melee calculator now lets each rule, including NicheAbility, apply in every fight or once across the series. Each fighter has independent settings.
- The shooting calculator offers the same choice for attack and defence rules in both situations. Unused triggered effects remain available for later shots, while the shared Saintly Relics limit and FNP are preserved.
- Frequency settings are saved in links, scenarios and weapon profiles. Existing settings keep their previous behaviour. For persistent rules, once mode limits the effect to the first fight or shot; explanations are available in Russian and English.

## 1.5.37 — 17 November 2026

- Administrators can add an existing player to an active team using player search in the management tab. The player joins immediately without accepting an invitation; pending invitations are closed and membership history is preserved.
- Current members are excluded from addition search results. Duplicate membership is prevented, and the administrator's action is recorded in the audit log.

## 1.5.36 — 17 November 2026

- Fixed personal podium awards for team tournaments: players in the top three rosters receive first-, second- and third-place medals in their profile Hall Of Fame. Awards use the roster at tournament completion.
- Missing personal medals are restored for completed team tournaments. Republishing results does not create duplicate awards, and team medals are preserved.

## 1.5.35 — 17 November 2026

- Fixed a Studio autosave race: newer edits are no longer replaced by an older snapshot or turned into unnecessary recovery copies by saves in the same tab. Edits made while a save is being acknowledged stay in the current draft.
- Added 0°, 90°, 180° and 270° dice logo rotation in the standalone D6 generator and Studio. The rotation is saved with the design and applied to previews, PNG files and new Tabletop Simulator exports.

## 1.5.34 — 17 November 2026

- Added a Strike Force Variel miniature to Tier Lists. Dragon Masters is now easy to find in the alphabetical catalog and uses the original Clanblade miniature.
- Replaced the Legionaries miniature with a Balefire Acolyte. All new images show the full model with a transparent background; existing rankings are preserved.
- The Variel card explains that tournament statistics are not available yet instead of showing a loading error.

## 1.5.33 — 16 November 2026

- Faction player leaderboards now require at least 10 eligible completed games on that faction across all time. Players with fewer games appear separately with a provisional Wilson score and no ranking place. This applies to faction statistics and Tier Lists.

## 1.5.32 — 16 November 2026

- Added the 2026 Q4 season starting on November 9. Results from that date count toward the new season, including games from tournaments that started earlier.
- Faction best-player lists now show an all-time Wilson score, accounting for win rate and sample size. The default order is highest Wilson score first, limited to five players. Recent games in faction details are limited to the latest five matches of the selected season.
- Added automatic personal faction achievements: Experienced at 50, Connoisseur at 60 and Master at 70 Wilson points. Each uses the player's entire history on that faction and is revoked when the score drops below its threshold.
- Profiles show only earned team training achievements. Game-card actions and statuses use a more compact layout and wrap when space is limited.

## 1.5.31 — 16 November 2026

## 1.5.30 — 16 November 2026

- Removed the redundant W / D / L suffix after numeric records on podium cards.
